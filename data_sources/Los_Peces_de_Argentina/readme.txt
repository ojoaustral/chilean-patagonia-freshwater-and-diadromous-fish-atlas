About Los Peces de Argentina:

Data downloaded from databasin.org

Data is coming from multiple sources, including museum collection and GBIF. But, most of the fish data coming from shared watershed with chile come from Fundación Oga, and the SSTN database. SSTN stands for "IABIN -Species and Specimens Thematic Network" (https://databasin.org/galleries/86ae7f941dca443f9d81086f01f94494/#expand=16047)

Institution acronyms stand for, FO = Fundación Oga

2024-04-2018:
The database "Los Peces de Argentina" is no longer available in Databasin. But it appears that all the records are also included in "Los Peces de Latinoamérica y el Caribe"
